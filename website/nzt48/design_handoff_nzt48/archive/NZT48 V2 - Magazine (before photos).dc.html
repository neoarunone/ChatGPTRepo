One capsule is the whole serving

“Nootropic” is a general term for ingredients people take to support focus, attention and mental energy. Many nootropic stacks ask for several capsules a day, or a handful of separate bottles.

NZT48 Original puts its 23-ingredient blend into one capsule. You take it once, with water and a meal, and that’s your serving for the day. Nothing to measure, mix or count out.

Your daily routine

Three steps · one capsule
  1. Step 1

    Take one capsule

    With 8oz of water, preferably with a meal, or as directed by a healthcare professional.

  2. Step 2

    Skip the extra caffeine

    NZT48 Original already contains caffeine. The label says to avoid other caffeinated products, including coffee and energy drinks.

  3. Step 3

    Take it earlier in the day

    Most people take it in the morning or with lunch, so the caffeine fits their day and their sleep.
