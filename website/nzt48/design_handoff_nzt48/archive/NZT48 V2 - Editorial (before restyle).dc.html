Nootropics, Explained

One Capsule Is The Whole Serving

“Nootropic” is a general term for ingredients people take to support focus, attention and mental energy. Many nootropic stacks ask for several capsules a day, or a handful of separate bottles.

NZT48 Original puts its 23-ingredient blend into one capsule. You take it once, with water and a meal, and that’s your serving for the day. Nothing to measure, mix or count out.
